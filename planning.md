# TakeMeter planning notes

## Milestone 1 — Community choice

**Community:** [`r/soccer`](https://www.reddit.com/r/soccer/), a public Reddit
community for discussion of soccer/football.

**Dataset scope:** Original, top-level comments on public `r/soccer`
discussion or news threads. I will exclude live match threads, bot posts,
deleted/removed text, and link-only comments. Top-level comments are complete,
standalone opinions, unlike replies that depend heavily on unseen context.

### Candidate distinctions observed

1. Some comments argue for a football claim using a checkable statistic,
   specific match event, transfer fee, date, or quote; others make the claim
   without evidence.
2. Some comments predict or evaluate a player, manager, club, or transfer with
   confident language but no supporting reason.
3. Some comments are immediate feelings about a recent result or announcement
   rather than an argument: celebration, frustration, disbelief, or mockery.

These are observations for the taxonomy-design milestone, not final labels.
